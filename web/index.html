✍️ Our numbers
Everyone picks together: tap a number to add it in your colour, tap it again to take it back. 🤖 asks the suggestion engine to pick for you.
How to read this. Every set of 6 numbers has exactly the same chance: 1 in 8,145,060, every week. We tested more than 1,200 real draws: the machine is fair, and hot, cold or overdue numbers predict nothing. What past results do show is which numbers people over-pick. When those numbers come up, more people win and the 1등–3등 prizes are split more ways. So the suggestions lean toward less-picked numbers, keep the usual shape of real winning draws (sum, odd/even, low/high, spread), and still give a small weight to how often each number came up. Score = how well the set fits all of that: 100 = this week's best set, 0 = a typical random set. Hover a score for its percentile. The suggestions stay the same all week and are the same for everyone.
